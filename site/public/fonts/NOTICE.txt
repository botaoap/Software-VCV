Self-hosted web fonts — VCV site
================================

Both files are the Latin-subset WOFF2 builds distributed by Google Fonts, self-hosted here
(real @font-face, not a third-party CDN: no visitor IP is sent to Google — LGPD).
Each is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL-1.1).

1. playfair-display-latin.woff2 — Playfair Display (variable, weight axis). Headings.
   Designer: Claus Eggers Sørensen · https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Playfair+Display
2. inter-latin.woff2 — Inter (variable, weight axis). Body / UI.
   Designer: Rasmus Andersson · https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter

Licence text: https://openfontlicense.org/ (travels with each font from its upstream source).
These are best-effort stand-ins for the brand faces still to be confirmed by the client.
